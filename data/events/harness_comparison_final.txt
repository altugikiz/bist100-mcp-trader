KEY FINDING
===========================================================================
The single most consequential harness design decision is not evidence
gating or state enforcement — it's simply forcing tool use. Version A
(no tools) hallucinated a completely different price level and
fabricated 2026 financials never returned by any tool; Version B (tools
only, no other safeguards) already eliminates numerical hallucination
entirely. Evidence completeness, state enforcement, and decision logging
(C→D→E) then add auditability and reproducibility on top of that
already-accurate foundation, rather than fixing accuracy problems
themselves.
